\section{Scope and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The recorded SAGA inconsistency propagates to token issuance and storage in
controlled executions. Under the corresponding fixed symbolic divergence,
\texttt{ChatAccept} is reachable while its prior-\texttt{TokenIssue}
correspondence holds. Token-origin consistency cannot substitute for
receiver-side authorization consistent with the intended policy.

The evidence covers one recorded case and the tested policies. It establishes
neither arbitrary-policy correctness nor an arbitrary-attacker or deployed
exploit. Only the symbolic model reaches message acceptance.
