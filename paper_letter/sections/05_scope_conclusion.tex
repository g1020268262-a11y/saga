\section{Scope and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Our evidence is limited to one recorded SAGA case and the tested policies; it
does not establish correctness for arbitrary policies or any Python--ProVerif
refinement. Empirical execution stops before message acceptance; no deployed
exploit is established. In controlled executions of the production consumer
paths, the inconsistency propagates to token issuance and storage. Separately,
\texttt{ChatAccept} is reachable in the symbolic model under the fixed divergence.
