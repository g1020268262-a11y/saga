\section{Scope and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Evidence covers one recorded SAGA case and tested policies, not arbitrary-policy
correctness or Python--ProVerif refinement. Empirical execution stops before
message acceptance; no deployed exploit is established. The matcher
inconsistency propagates to token issuance/storage in controlled production
consumers; separately, symbolic \texttt{ChatAccept} is reachable under the
fixed divergence.
