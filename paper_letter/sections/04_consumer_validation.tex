\section{Consumer-Side Propagation Validation}
\label{sec:consumer-propagation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

\paragraph{Purpose.}
We test whether production consumers propagate matcher decisions to token
issuance/storage, independently of Section~\ref{sec:formalization}.

\paragraph{Environment.}
The harness executes production matcher calls in Provider \texttt{/access} and
the receiver handler, with OTK release/consumption, token generation, and
\texttt{active\_\allowbreak tokens} storage. Fixtures replace registration,
certificate services, and TLS transport. Execution stops only at
\texttt{receive\_conversation}, after token sending and storage.

\paragraph{Experiments.}
Provider and receiver use identical policies. With specific pattern $s$ and
wildcard $w$, the tested policies are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\mathrm{A/A} & [(s,10),(w,10)] \\
\mathrm{D/D} & [(w,10),(s,-1)] \\
\mathrm{D/A} & [(s,-1),(w,10)] .
\end{array}
\]
Labels are specification/observed implementation decisions; each case ran
once with fresh state and one OTK.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\hline
Spec./impl. & Provider HTTP & OTK released & Token sent/stored \\
\hline
A/A & 200 & Yes & Yes \\
D/D & 403 & No & Not reached \\
D/A & 200 & Yes & Yes \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Consumer propagation results; message acceptance was not executed.}
\label{tab:consumer-propagation}
\end{table}

A/A is the positive control. D/D is Provider-blocking: HTTP~403 leaves state
unchanged and never reaches the receiver, so it does not test receiver
rejection. D/A tests inconsistency propagation.

\paragraph{Result and evidence.}
In D/A, the specification selects $-1$. The Provider matcher returns $10$;
HTTP~200 releases its OTK and initializes counter $9$. The receiver processes
the request, returns $10$, consumes the OTK, and generates, sends, and stores
one token in \texttt{active\_tokens}.

The empirical endpoint is token issuance/storage, not message acceptance:
no application message is sent. No \texttt{receive\_conversation} logic runs.
Neither \texttt{local\_agent.run}, LLM calls, nor tool execution occurs.
