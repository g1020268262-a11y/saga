\section{Consumer-Side Propagation Validation}
\label{sec:consumer-propagation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

\paragraph{Purpose.}
We test matcher-decision propagation through production consumers to token
issuance/storage; Section~\ref{sec:formalization} analyzes symbolic acceptance.

\paragraph{Environment.}
The harness runs production Provider \texttt{/access}, matcher, receiver
handler, token generation, and \texttt{active\_\allowbreak tokens} storage
against isolated MongoDB. Fixtures replace registration, certificate services,
and transport. \texttt{receive\_\allowbreak conversation} stops execution
after token sending/storage.

\paragraph{Experiments.}
Provider and receiver use identical policies. With specific pattern $s$ and
wildcard $w$, the tested policies are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\mathrm{A/A} & [(s,10),(w,10)] \\
\mathrm{D/D} & [(w,10),(s,-1)] \\
\mathrm{D/A} & [(s,-1),(w,10)] .
\end{array}
\]
Labels are specification/observed implementation decisions; each case ran
once with fresh state and one OTK.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\hline
Spec./impl. & Provider HTTP & OTK released & Token sent/stored \\
\hline
A/A & 200 & Yes & Yes \\
D/D & 403 & No & Not reached \\
D/A & 200 & Yes & Yes \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Consumer propagation results; message acceptance was not executed.}
\label{tab:consumer-propagation}
\end{table}

A/A is the positive control for token issuance and storage. D/D is the
Provider-blocking control: HTTP~403 leaves state unchanged; the receiver is
not reached. D/A tests inconsistency propagation.

\paragraph{Result and evidence.}
In D/A, the specification selects $-1$; both matcher calls return $10$.
The Provider returns HTTP~200, releases its OTK, and initializes counter $9$.
The receiver consumes the OTK and generates, sends, and stores one token in
\texttt{active\_tokens}. Artifacts are archived for reproducibility.

No application message, LLM call, or tool execution occurs; the endpoint is
token issuance/storage.
