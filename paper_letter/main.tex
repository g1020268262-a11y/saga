\begin{summary}
We analyze an order-dependent authorization inconsistency in SAGA:
a later wildcard-allow rule can replace a more-specific deny because the matcher
fails to retain the best pattern. In controlled executions, the implementation
decision propagates through production consumer paths to token issuance and
storage. Under the fixed divergence, ProVerif reports \texttt{ChatAccept}
reachable; separately, it verifies a non-injective correspondence requiring
prior \texttt{TokenIssue}.
\end{summary}
