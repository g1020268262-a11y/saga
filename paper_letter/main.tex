\begin{summary}
Authentication does not guarantee compliance with a receiver's contact policy.
We identify an order-dependent SAGA matcher defect converting specified denial
into permission. Controlled Provider/receiver executions confirm propagation
to token issuance and storage. In an evidence-linked symbolic scenario,
ProVerif reports conditional \texttt{ChatAccept} reachability while the
prior-\texttt{TokenIssue} correspondence holds, separating token origin from
policy compliance.
\end{summary}
