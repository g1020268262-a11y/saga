\begin{summary}
We analyze an authorization decision inconsistency in SAGA. A formal coverage
diagnostic first shows that existing authentication and token-secrecy properties
do not establish a required authorization correspondence. Implementation
analysis then identifies an order-dependent matcher defect whose decision
propagates through production consumers to token issuance and storage. Under the
fixed divergence, ProVerif reports \texttt{ChatAccept} reachable; separately,
its non-injective prior-\texttt{TokenIssue} correspondence is true.
\end{summary}
