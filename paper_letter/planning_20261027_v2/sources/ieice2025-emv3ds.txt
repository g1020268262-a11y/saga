2. EMV 3-D Secure
In this section, we explain the details of EMV 3-D Secure.
There are several versions of EMV 3-D Secure, and this
paper is based on Version 2.3.1.1 (2023.05.30).

6.1 Consideration of Impact of Our Findings on Real-
World Systems
If our relaxed scheme that does not require a unilateral au-
thenticated channel is adopted in an actual system, it is ex-
pected to reduce latency in establishing the communication
channel. Our scheme can simply be realized by omitting
the establishment of the secure channel between the card-
holder and the ACS in the implementation. In the speciﬁca-
tion [31], App-basedCReq/CRescommunication shall use
TLS 1.2 or higher. At ISPEC 2022, Tasopoulos et al. [35]
presentedareportontheimplementationofTLS1.3(includ-
ing post-quantum cryptographic primitives). Their evalua-
tionindicatesthattheECDSA-ECDHEhandshake,whichis
currently typical for TLS1.3, has a communication volume
of about 2,300 bytes, which results in a latency of about
100 milliseconds. In addition, NIST [36] plans to transi-
tion to post-quantum cryptography in 2030, and it has been
revealed that Dilithium-Kyber, which is equivalent to NIST
Level3,hasacommunicationvolumeofabout20,000bytes
and a latency of about 150 milliseconds in TLS 1.3. On
the other hand, CNP transactions are executed daily around
the world, and data exist showing that in 2022, for example
in Singapore, about 280 million transactions were executed
annually [37]. If it is possible to reduce the number of TLS
1.3 connections established in the Challenge Flow in EMV
3-D Secure, which is executed a large number of times, it
is expected to reduce latency and server load in the Internet

7. Conclusion
In this paper, we presented the ﬁrst formal veriﬁcation for
Challenge Flow in EMV 3-D Secure. We have shown that
all of the veriﬁed models of Challenge Flow satisfy all of
the following security requirements: conﬁdentiality and re-
sistance to oﬀ-line dictionary attacks of secret information,
and authenticity and resistance to replay attacks of the card-
holder, for both the man-in-the-middle attacker model and
thecolludingattackermodelwithrelayservers(3DSServer,
DS). In addition, we clariﬁed that, in App-based, even if the
protocol is made more eﬃcient without assuming a unilat-
eral authenticated channel, it does not aﬀect the security of
the protocol.
Althoughthispaperonlyfocusesonverifyingthespec-
iﬁcations,itisalsointerestingtoinvestigatesecurityinareas
outside of the speciﬁcations for EMV 3-D Secure, depend-
ingonspeciﬁcimplementationsofapplicationsorbrowsers.
For example, do the services oﬀered by each international
brand really follow the speciﬁcations? Also, can malicious
browsersstealthesecretinformationenteredbyauser? Fur-
thermore, could a website be created that mimics the EMV
3-D Secure’s authentication screen so that users could enter
their secret information? Thus, we leave a remaining prob-
lem for not only security focused on protocol speciﬁcations
but also security focused on actual use in future work.
